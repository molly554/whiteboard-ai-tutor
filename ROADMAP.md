# Roadmap

## v0.2.0 — Compare explanations

- Filter the existing boards by teaching scenario, model, review verdict and thinking setting.
- Compare two models on the same question, with finished boards and review notes side by side.
- Open each recording independently, return to the comparison and share the selected question and models.

This release uses the existing recordings and scores. See [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## v0.1.0 — Demo and discovery

An animation-first gallery, responsive playback, English/Chinese interface continuity, and clearer project and benchmark information.

## Under evaluation: PDF export

Exporting step-end boards together with their narration is being evaluated in [#9](https://github.com/molly554/whiteboard-ai-tutor/issues/9). Scope and timing are not committed.

## Research directions

Higher-level geometry operations ([#7](https://github.com/molly554/whiteboard-ai-tutor/issues/7)), screenshot-based correction,
evaluation against human judgments, speech narration and mid-stroke interruption remain separate experiments.
Manual editing, undo and restoration are being evaluated in [#3](https://github.com/molly554/whiteboard-ai-tutor/issues/3).
The frozen v1 benchmark remains available for reproducibility.
