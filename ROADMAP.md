# Roadmap

## v0.1.0 — Demo and discovery

An animation-first gallery, responsive playback, English/Chinese interface continuity, and clearer project and
benchmark information. See [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Next: v0.2.0 — Compare explanations

- Filter the existing recordings by teaching scenario, model and review verdict.
- Compare two models on the same question, with the finished boards and review notes side by side.
- Open each recording independently to inspect the drawing process.
- Share a link that preserves the selected question and models.

This iteration uses existing recordings and scores. It does not require new model calls or change the historical leaderboard.

## Research directions

Higher-level geometry operations, screenshot-based correction, evaluation against human judgments, speech narration
and mid-stroke interruption remain experiments to investigate separately.
